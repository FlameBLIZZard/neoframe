Questions, answered

Something else on your mind? Add it as a note when you join the waitlist and we'll reply by email.

When does NEOFRAME launch?+

We're opening access in waves through 2026. Join the waitlist and you'll get an email when your spot opens — waitlist members go first.

Will there be a free plan?+

Yes. The Free plan includes 100+ templates, the core editor and standard exports, with no card needed.

Can I use my designs commercially?+

Designs made on Pro and Team come with a commercial licence for client work, merch, ads and digital products.

Is there a student discount?+

Yes — verified students get 50% off Pro. Pick "Student" when you join the waitlist and we'll send the details at launch.

Which file formats can I export?+

PNG, JPG, SVG, and print-ready PDF with bleed and crop marks.

Can my whole team work on one design?+

Yes. Team plans include live co-editing, a shared brand kit, font uploads and role-based permissions.
